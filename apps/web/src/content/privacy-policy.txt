2. Information collected and purposes

Account and profile: registration processes your name, password, date of birth, gender and at least one account identifier (email, mobile number or CloudComAI User ID). These support account creation, authentication, adult eligibility, profiles and finding people. The current registration service requires gender and date of birth; a profile photo and interests are additional information you may provide. Passwords are stored as password hashes. Email is used for password recovery and, where configured, account verification and service messages.

Conversations and activity: we store message text, recipients, group membership, replies, edits, polls and votes, saved messages, delivery/read state, timestamps, online presence and notification preferences to deliver and synchronize conversations and provide the controls you use. Public-room reports and moderation records include the reporting and reported accounts, room/message references, reasons, details and action history. Story/status features also process content, audience and view records. Call setup, where available, uses caller and recipient identifiers, call type, status and timestamps. Voice messages you record and send are uploaded media as described below.

Media: photos, videos, voice recordings, documents, filenames, file size, media type and associated metadata are uploaded when you send them or update your profile/group image. Files and message content are stored on our servers. Uploaded files may contain embedded information; remove information you do not want to share before uploading.

Contacts: optional phone contact sync uploads names and the selected email address and phone number from contacts accessible to the app. We store this information to match registered CloudComAI users and display your contacts. Contacts can include people who have not joined CloudComAI. Sync only contacts you are entitled to provide. Opening the contacts list alone does not authorize a new phone-address-book upload.

Google Contacts: if you connect Google, we request read-only contacts access and basic Google account identity information. We store contact names, email addresses, phone numbers and Google contact identifiers, your connected Google email/account identifier, an encrypted refresh token and sync metadata. Connected contacts may be refreshed when you use People & Contacts as well as when you choose Sync. Gmail messages are not requested. Section 5 describes Google-specific restrictions and revocation.

Location: when you choose location sharing, coordinates and the associated chat/account are sent to our server and recipients. Coordinates may reveal your precise position even if the device provides reduced accuracy. A city or language room selection is also stored as membership; it is not proof of your physical location. The current mobile location button requests foreground location, not continuous background tracking. Any separate live-location record has its own stop/expiry state; stopping it is not the same as erasing the stored record or a location message already sent.

Devices and security: authentication/session records, device labels, push tokens, platform information, IP addresses, browser/user-agent information where recorded, access times and server errors support sign-in, delivery, reliability and security. The app does not require an advertising identifier for messaging. Hosting providers also process connection data when serving requests.

Local storage and backups: browser/device storage keeps sign-in state, preferences, cached messages, drafts and pending sends. The mobile app uses secure device storage for credentials and its optional app-lock PIN. Cloud account backup, when configured and enabled, stores an encrypted server snapshot of profile/settings, contacts, available conversations and included media. A downloaded account export contains readable personal information; protect it and any device or service to which you share it.

Support: information you submit in support, privacy and safety requests is used to verify, investigate, respond to and record the outcome of the request.

3. Permissions and your choices

Camera access is used to take a photo or video you choose to upload. Microphone access is used for voice or video recording. Photo/document selection gives the app the files you select. Contacts access enables the optional upload described above. Location access enables sharing your selected position. Notification permission enables device alerts. Declining an optional permission affects that feature; it does not authorize us to collect that information by another route.

You can change device permissions in system settings and notification, profile-visibility and presence preferences in CloudComAI. Revoking a device permission stops future access allowed by that permission; it does not erase information already uploaded. Contact us to request removal of stored information. Terms acceptance does not grant permission for contacts, location, recordings, advertising or future AI processing.

4. Who can receive information

Other users receive messages and files you send them, group/public-room content you post, your name, account identifier and profile image, and profile fields allowed by the applicable visibility controls. Current profile defaults can show age and gender, while email/mobile sharing is off by default. Public rooms are discoverable by other registered users. Recipients can save, forward, export or capture content; message expiry cannot remove their independently held copies.

Our hosting, database, storage and email delivery providers process the information needed to run the service. Optional mobile push delivery uses Expo's push service and the relevant Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service. Delivery includes a push token, notification routing data and, when previews are enabled, a notification title and message excerpt. Notifications can appear on a locked device. You can disable previews or notifications in the available settings. Expo update services may receive technical device/app information in builds where over-the-air updates are enabled.

Google processes your authorization and the Google Contacts API requests. External map links send the selected coordinates and ordinary browser connection information to the map provider when you open the link. External services have their own notices for their independent processing.

We require providers acting for us to restrict processing to the stated service purposes and to provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable privacy obligations. We may disclose limited information when legally required, to address fraud or security incidents, or to establish or defend legal rights. Any business transfer must respect existing privacy restrictions; the stricter Google-data rules in section 5 continue to apply.

5. Google API data: restricted purposes

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy

Google data is used for the connected contacts and contact-matching features you authorize. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, transfer it to data brokers, use it to decide creditworthiness, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Transfers are limited to delivering the authorized features with consent, security, legal obligations, or a permitted business transfer with the required prior consent. Human access is limited to your affirmative permission for specific data, necessary security/legal work, or permitted aggregated internal operations.

You can revoke CloudComAI access from your Google Account connections page: https://myaccount.google.com/connections . Revocation prevents further authorized Google access; imported copies and backups are separate. Request their deletion from support@cloudcomai.com. Removing CloudComAI does not delete your Google Account or the original contacts held by Google.

6. Advertising, tracking and future features

The reviewed messaging service has no advertising or cross-service behavioral advertising integration and no general-purpose AI processing integration. We do not use your private messages or contacts to train general-purpose AI models. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising in this service. Sending a message to another user is distinct from advertising-related sharing under US privacy laws.

Essential browser/device storage supports authentication, preferences and messaging. We do not change this essential processing in response to a browser Do Not Track signal. There is no advertising sale/sharing activity in the service for a Global Privacy Control signal to opt out of. External websites you choose to visit may have different practices. Before adding tracking, ads, new analytics, AI or payments, we will provide the relevant notice and obtain consent or provide opt-out controls where required; this policy is not blanket consent to future features.

7. Retention and deletion

Account/profile information and imported contacts remain in the service while needed for your account and the associated purposes, subject to deletion requests and applicable retention obligations. Account uninstall, sign-out, chat removal from your list, contact permission withdrawal and account deletion are different actions.

New private and group messages normally default to 30-day expiry; public-room messages normally default to four hours. Conversation settings or administrator configuration may change these periods, and older records may have different or no expiry settings. Polls default to 30 days when a closing time is not supplied. Expired messages cease to be available through the normal message queries; physical removal of records and attachment files depends on the cleanup job completing successfully. These defaults are not a promise that every copy disappears at the exact expiry instant.

Deleting a message for yourself hides your copy. Delete for everyone affects the service's conversation copy, subject to available controls. Neither action erases independent recipient downloads, exports or screenshots. Screenshot alerts cannot detect or prevent all captures.

The latest cloud backup is retained until replaced or removed; disabling automatic backups does not erase the existing backup. Exported copies remain wherever you save or share them. Restoring a backup does not authorize revival of expired/deleted messages. Backups, moderation records, session records, technical logs and legal holds require separate retention handling from message expiry.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information at support@cloudcomai.com, including if you no longer have the app. We may reasonably verify control of the account. Any data retained for a specific legal, security or fraud-prevention reason must be identified with its purpose and applicable retention period. Contacting support is the current request channel; this notice does not represent that an automated in-app account-deletion feature exists.

8. Access, correction, withdrawal and complaints

You can use profile/privacy controls to change available information and preferences and use account export where offered. An account backup/export is not necessarily a complete response to a legal access request. Contact support@cloudcomai.com for access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, information about disclosures, or a complaint, specifying your request and account identifier. Verification should be proportionate to the request; we do not need your password.

Withdrawal affects future processing that relies on consent and may make a requested feature unavailable. It does not undo earlier lawful processing. Optional features remain separate from account acceptance. Where Indian law grants them and the relevant provisions apply, rights include access, correction/completion/update, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination of another person. You may use the competent statutory complaint or appeal mechanism when available; this notice does not remove those rights.

10. Adults and children's privacy

CloudComAI accounts are restricted to users aged 18 or older. We do not offer a child account or parental-consent enrollment service. The date of birth entered at registration is used to enforce that restriction. An adult-only statement does not excuse processing children's information unlawfully. If you believe an account belongs to a minor, or a child's data has been improperly submitted, contact support@cloudcomai.com so the information and account can be investigated and restricted or removed as required. Do not send illegal images with a report.

11. Security and international processing

The service uses authenticated access, password hashing and secure transport when accessed over HTTPS. Google refresh tokens and configured cloud backup files are encrypted with server-controlled keys. Messages are stored in a form the server can process; CloudComAI messaging is not end-to-end encrypted. An app-lock PIN does not make server messages end-to-end encrypted. No service can guarantee absolute security or prevention of screenshots.

Information may be processed where CloudComAI and its hosting, communications and other providers operate, including outside your country. Applicable transfer restrictions and safeguards must be respected. For information about the providers and processing locations for your account, contact support@cloudcomai.com. A US expansion does not itself mean all data will be stored in the US. If a breach requires notification, affected people and authorities will be notified according to applicable law.

12. Changes

We will update the revision/effective date when this policy changes. Material changes will be brought to users' attention through an appropriate in-app notice or other direct communication, and new consent will be requested where required before new processing starts. Continuing to use the service does not replace consent or an opt-out right required by law. Mandatory consumer and privacy rights continue to apply.
