# CloudComAI privacy, terms and store-readiness review

Reviewed 18 September 2026. Scope: web, Android/iOS shared app, PHP API, database definitions, retention/backup/push code and dependencies in `cloudcomai/cloudcomai-platform`. The review began at main `9f41d683` and incorporated main `9716d416`, preserving the subsequently merged web and mobile fixes, including mobile-layout PR #123. Its chat-composer changes are separate from this PR's registration edits. This review includes India and planned US expansion.

**The revised policies are review drafts. Do not publish or resubmit them with the owner placeholders unresolved.** Text changes alone will not resolve the account-deletion and safety-process gaps below. Apple/Google rejection messages, production infrastructure, developer-console answers, provider contracts and installed release binaries were not available. Findings describe repository evidence; they are not a determination of the actual rejection reason or a guarantee of legal compliance. An India/US privacy lawyer should confirm applicability and the final operator commitments before publication.

## What this change delivers

- Revised Privacy Policy and Terms of Service tied to the implemented features, including a US supplement, Google API Limited Use, contact uploads, location, public audiences, notification previews, server-readable messages, backups, retention exceptions, rights requests and safety reporting.
- Standalone public HTML privacy, terms and account-deletion information pages, plus shared offline legal documents in mobile. Links appear at signup/login and settings, and in the web footer.
- Registration notice and explicit mobile terms/age acknowledgment, matching the existing web acknowledgment. This is not consent to every optional data use.
- A separate phone-contact upload disclosure and affirmative choice. Opening or refreshing People & Contacts no longer initiates phone-address-book sync. The permission call now uses Expo's module-level API; the old call incorrectly treated it as a Contact-class method. Google connection notices explain imports and subsequent refreshes.
- A corrected web export description: account exports can embed media and contain readable personal information.
- One canonical text source, generated public/mobile copies, and regression tests for parity and contact-consent ordering.

## Apple, Google Play and Google OAuth

Google Play requires a public, accessible privacy page with app/developer identity, contact, data handling, retention and deletion information, plus an in-app link. Unexpected sensitive-data collection requires a prominent disclosure and consent before access. Account-creating apps need an in-app deletion request route and a web route for users without the app. The HTML and contact changes address part of this; a support page alone does not finish the required product functionality. [Google Play user-data policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311?hl=en), [account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en).

Apple requires the privacy-policy link in App Store Connect and the app. Its account-deletion guidance generally disallows making ordinary apps use email/customer support alone; deletion must be initiated within the app. User-generated-content apps also need appropriate filtering, reporting, blocking and contact arrangements. [App Review Guidelines, 1.2 and 5.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Apple account-deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/).

Use the inventory above to complete Play Data safety and Apple's privacy labels, including third-party SDK behavior and whether data is linked to a user. Do not choose “no data collected.” Collection for functionality is different from tracking; do not request App Tracking Transparency simply because contacts exist. Verify tracking in the release binary before answering. [Apple app privacy details](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

Google OAuth verification is separate from Google Play review. Confirm the rejection source, authorized domain, branding and privacy URL. Keep scopes limited to the actual contacts feature, and ensure processor access, transfers and human access follow the commitments added to the draft. [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy).

Assess Play's Social/Dating child-safety standards even with an 18+ audience: published standards, in-app feedback, action on known abuse material, lawful reporting and a designated child-safety contact require real operation. Terms prohibitions alone are insufficient. [Google Play child-safety standards](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14747720).

## India: current duties and phased preparation

The IT intermediary rules require published rules/privacy terms and grievance arrangements where applicable. The February 2026 amendments shorten key deadlines: ordinary grievance resolution to seven days, specified complaints to 36 hours, intimate-content/impersonation action to two hours, and qualifying court/government-order action to three hours. Acknowledgment remains 24 hours. They also change the user-rules reminder interval to at least every three months. Counsel should classify each request and assess synthetic-media obligations; an email address without triage/staffing is inadequate. [2023 consolidated rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Information-Technology-Intermediary-Guidelines-and-Digital-Media-Ethics-Code-Rules-2021-updated-06.04.2023-.pdf), [February 2026 amendment, effective 20 February](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/f55fe52418b03f58b0669f6a8bc03b6d.pdf).

The final DPDP Rules 2025 have phased commencement. Rule 4 starts after one year; most substantive rules, including rules 3 and 5–16, after 18 months from publication, broadly May 2027. Do not describe all of them as already operative in September 2026. Prepare itemized notices, withdrawal/rights handling, child-data controls, nomination, security/breach procedures and deletion now; verify the matching Act commencement and applicable exemptions. Rule 14's maximum 90-day period concerns grievance response, not a universal deadline for every privacy request. [Official DPDP Rules gazette, rule 1 and rule 14](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).

Separately assess continuing IT Act/SPDI requirements for the operator's current processing, including passwords, consent, security and transfers. Review CERT-In incident-reporting and logging duties against the actual entity and infrastructure; a generic breach paragraph is not an incident-response procedure. The underlying CERT-In PDF was not retrievable during this review, so its detailed deadlines have not been independently restated here. [CERT-In official directions and FAQs](https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp).

## US expansion: applicability and actions

| Requirement | Applicability/action for CloudComAI |
|---|---|
| CalOPPA public notice | A commercial service collecting covered information from California consumers can have notice duties below CCPA business-size thresholds. Publish categories/recipients, effective date, change process, available access/correction process and truthful Do Not Track/cross-site tracking disclosures. [California Business and Professions Code 22575](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=22575.) |
| CCPA/CPRA | Assess the business definition and current inflation-adjusted revenue, data-volume, revenue-from-sale/sharing and related-entity tests. If covered, finalize notice at collection, required historical disclosures, access/correction/deletion/portability, agent requests and applicable opt-out/sensitive-data controls. The draft supplement is not a finding of coverage. [California DOJ guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) |
| Other state comprehensive laws | Build a state-by-state applicability register from intended markets, actual users, entity size/exemptions and processing. Texas illustrates why California thresholds cannot be reused: most SBA-defined small businesses are exempt there, with a sensitive-data-sale exception. Covered processing includes notice, request/appeal handling, processor contracts, sensitive-data consent and relevant assessments. [Texas Attorney General guidance](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-privacy-rights/texas-data-privacy-and-security-act) |
| Sale/targeted advertising/GPC | No such integration was found. Verify SDKs/contracts and historical practices before asserting this. If introduced, implement applicable opt-outs, recognized signals and provider propagation; accepting terms is insufficient. GPC is distinct from legacy Do Not Track. [California DOJ GPC guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa/gpc) |
| Children | COPPA applies to child-directed services and relevant actual knowledge of under-13 collection; an 18+ term alone does not decide applicability. Use the current amended rule and design a minor-account investigation/removal route. State youth rules need separate applicability/current-litigation review if minors are reached. [Current 16 CFR Part 312](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312) |
| Intimate-image removal | Since 19 May 2026, covered platforms must offer an accessible notice process and act on valid requests within 48 hours, including reasonable efforts concerning known identical copies. This can cover messaging services. Implement nonuser access and urgent escalation; five reports cannot be a prerequisite. [FTC TAKE IT DOWN Act guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act) |
| Copyright | If relying on applicable DMCA safe harbors, register and publicly identify the designated agent and operate notice/counter-notice and repeat-infringer procedures. A generic support address is not proof of registration. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/) |
| Assessments and security | Review applicable risk-assessment, cybersecurity-audit and automated-decision rules before new sensitive or high-risk processing. California's 2026 rules include conditional and phased obligations; they are not a requirement for every small app to immediately perform every audit. [CPPA regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/ccpa_updates.html) |

Use a staffed privacy-request register with jurisdiction-specific verification, response, extension, appeal and record-retention rules. Do not promise one blanket deadline for all US requests or breaches. Ordinary privacy promises and security practices also need legal review; “we do not sell” does not excuse inaccurate notice or inadequate protection. Expansion planning must include state breach-notification laws, vendor contracts and actual hosting/transfer arrangements, beyond changes to the policy.

## Implementation backlog and owner decisions

1. **Before publication:** provide legal operator, contact address, named grievance officer, monitored support/urgent channels and effective date. Confirm providers, processing countries, contractual restrictions, all data recipients and no-sale/advertising/AI claims. Obtain the exact Apple/Google rejection notices and current console answers.
2. **Before store resubmission:** implement and test account deletion across accounts, sessions, push tokens, imported contacts/OAuth credentials, content/media, polls/votes, stories, backups and third-party processors. Specify any lawful limited retention. Add public request tracking/confirmation and a deletion-completion period the service can meet.
3. **Before US launch:** complete state applicability analysis using business status, revenue, user/data volumes, intended states and any sale/sharing history; implement rights/appeals and urgent-image removal operations. Determine DMCA registration strategy and child-safety/reporting responsibilities.
4. **Privacy engineering:** persist versioned acceptance without bundling optional consents, improve defaults/minimization, add Google/contact deletion, finish contextual permission disclosures, review location/call authorization and test blocked/nonmember cases.
5. **Retention operations:** document separate periods for each table/file family, moderation/legal holds, backups, provider copies, access/security logs and deletion-request evidence. Verify cron runs, retries and monitoring; prevent restore from resurrecting deleted/expired data.
6. **Release review:** resolve owner placeholders, have the final legal text reviewed, rebuild native binaries where permission configuration changes, inspect privacy manifests/SDK network behavior, update console disclosures and validate the public URLs from a logged-out browser.

## Files, publishing and validation

Canonical text: `apps/web/src/content/privacy-policy.txt`, `terms-of-service.txt`, `account-deletion.txt`, and `registration-notice.txt`. Run `node scripts/generate-legal.mjs` after changes; `node scripts/generate-legal.mjs --check` detects stale copies and runs in the legal-document regression test.

Generated public files are `privacy-policy.html`, `terms-of-service.html` and `account-deletion.html` under `apps/web/public`. With a `/app/` deployment base, proposed URLs are `/app/privacy-policy.html`, `/app/terms-of-service.html` and `/app/account-deletion.html`; root deployment uses the corresponding root paths. These are build outputs, not a claim that the URLs are deployed or accepted. Use the actual verified HTTPS URLs in both store consoles and Google OAuth configuration.

Validation covers the JavaScript regression suite, API contract verification, web production build and Expo Android/iOS bundle export. Native device behavior, production email/delete operations, store-console declarations and external legal applicability remain outside automated checks. No database schema change is included in this PR; account deletion and acceptance persistence need separately designed changes, with both upgrade migrations and fresh-install schema updates if required.
